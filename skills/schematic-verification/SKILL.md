# Schematic Verification

Check device count, masters, all eight CDF fields, `totalM=NF*M`, local-stub labels, absence of direct device wires, VDC values, output-pin endpoints, floating-net warnings, and Check and Save result. Separate generator, schematic, DC-operating-point, and performance verification. Promote an artifact only with the exact dated evidence and updated runbook/regression ledger.
