# Topology Selection

Provide a device/net table containing MOS type, function, gate, drain, source, bulk, external ports, and VDC-driven nets. Identify CMFB and bias strategy. Treat repeated net labels as logical connectivity; require the terminal-to-local-stub-to-label routing contract in the approved topology summary.
